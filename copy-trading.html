Copy Trading

Copy trading lets your account automatically repeat the trades of another trader. It is run by Deriv, not by this site, so you set it up in your Deriv account.

How It Works

3 steps
STEP 1 CHOOSE

Pick a Trader

Look at a trader's history, how long they have traded, and how big their losing periods were, not only their best month.

STEP 2 LIMIT

Set Your Limits

Decide the most you will put in, the maximum stake per trade, and when copying should stop.

STEP 3 WATCH

Monitor and Stop

Check results regularly. You can stop copying at any time, but trades already opened still finish.

Before You Copy

Risks

You can still lose

A trader with strong past results can have a bad run. Your account takes the same losses they do.

Delays change results

Your trades may open at slightly different prices than theirs, so your results can differ from theirs.

Check availability

Copy trading features and country availability are set by Deriv and can change, so confirm in your Deriv account.
