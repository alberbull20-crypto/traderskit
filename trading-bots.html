Pick a bot and press Run Bot after logging in.

Bots place real trades on your Deriv account. Each tick on a volatility index is random, so no pattern or setting gives a bot an edge, and Deriv pays less than fair odds, so winning often does not mean profit. Martingale makes small wins common and rare losses large. The bot stops at your take profit, stop loss or trade limit, and it will not accept a stake above 10 or a stop loss above 50. Risk warning: you can lose your whole stake. Test on a demo account first, and only trade with money you can afford to lose.
